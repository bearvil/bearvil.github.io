Bookmark-Tab Title

Tabs · Bookmark names as tab titles

Browser extension Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

Your tabs, named your way

When the page in a tab is one of your bookmarks, the tab shows the bookmark's name instead of the page title. Rename a bookmark to “Mail – work” and the tab stays “Mail – work”, even when the page keeps changing its own title.

  • ✓Choose per bookmark how far the name reaches: one address, a section or the whole site
  • ✓Tells several accounts apart, such as Google's /u/1/ or authuser=1
  • ✓Set it from the toolbar popup, the settings page or the right-click menu
