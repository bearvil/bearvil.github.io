About

The studio behind Bearvil

An independent software studio of one. No investors, no growth hacks — just apps and tools worth keeping.

Our story

Built by one person, on purpose

Bearvil started as a way to build the apps big studios wouldn't: calm, private, and finished. No growth team, no A/B-tested dark patterns — just software that respects the person using it.

Every app ships small and gets better slowly. If a feature doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship.

When this site says “we”, it means the studio: one developer doing the design, the code, the testing and every reply to your email.

What we believe

Principles we don't compromise on

Calm by default

No push notifications begging for attention. Apps should fit into your day, not hijack it.

Privacy first

Your data stays yours. No accounts, no trackers, no ad networks, no quietly-added analytics SDKs.

No dark patterns

No fake urgency, no confusing cancel flows, no guilt-tripping copy. Ever.

Small and sustainable

A handful of focused apps and tools, maintained for years — not a pile of abandoned experiments.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach us at bearvil.info@gmail.com.

Who's behind Bearvil?
Bearvil is an independent, one-person software studio. No agency and no outsourcing — every app and tool is designed, built and supported by the same person.
Do your apps use AI?
Frend AI is an AI chat app: it connects straight from your device to the AI provider you choose, with your own API key. There is no Bearvil server in between, and we never see your messages or keys. Touchy Fingies and Dicty have no AI and no internet access at all, and Bookmark-Tab Title makes no network requests.
Do you collect any of my data?
No. Bearvil runs no servers, accounts or analytics, so there is nothing for us to collect. Each app's privacy policy spells out exactly what stays on your device and what (if anything) leaves it.
When can I download your apps and tools?
Frend AI, Touchy Fingies and Dicty are in final testing and coming soon to Google Play, and Bookmark-Tab Title is coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. See our projects for details.
Can I request a feature or report a bug?
Always. Email bearvil.info@gmail.com — every message is read, and a small studio can move fast on a good idea.
Where can I find your privacy policies?
Here: Frend AI, Touchy Fingies, Dicty and Bookmark-Tab Title. They are also in the Privacy Policies menu at the top of every page.
