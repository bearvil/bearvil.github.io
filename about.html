About

The studio behind Bearvil

A small, independent app studio. No investors, no growth hacks — just apps we'd want to use ourselves.

Our story

Built by one person, on purpose

Bearvil started as a way to build the apps big studios wouldn't: calm, private, and finished. No growth team, no A/B-tested dark patterns — just software that respects the person using it.

Every app ships small and gets better slowly. If a feature doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship.

What we believe

Principles we don't compromise on

Calm by default

No push notifications begging for attention. Apps should fit into your day, not hijack it.

Privacy first

Your data stays yours. No trackers, no ad networks, no quietly-added analytics SDKs.

No dark patterns

No fake urgency, no confusing cancel flows, no guilt-tripping copy. Ever.

Small and sustainable

A handful of focused apps, maintained for years — not a pile of abandoned experiments.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach us at bearvil.co@gmail.com.

Who's behind Bearvil?
Bearvil is an independent one-person app studio. No agency, no outsourced design — every app is built end-to-end in-house.
Do your apps use AI?
Some do, like Frend AI — and where they do, it's on-device or clearly disclosed. AI is never used to track you or sell your data.
How do you make money without ads?
Through optional one-time purchases and upgrades — never subscriptions you forget about or ads that sell your attention.
Can I request a feature or report a bug?
Always. Email us at bearvil.co@gmail.com — every message is read, and small studios move fast on good ideas.
Where can I find your privacy policies?
Every app's privacy policy is linked from the Privacy Policies menu in the top navigation.
